BPUT PS10 - 
Problem Statement: CAMPUSLINK - AI Powered
Campus-to-Corporate Placement Management &
Analytics Platform
Domain: EdTech + Artificial Intelligence / Placement & Career Services Automation
Background
College and university placement cells are responsible for managing thousands of students,
hundreds of recruiters and dozens of overlapping placement drives every academic season.
Most placement cells still rely on scattered spreadsheets, email chains, WhatsApp groups
and manual shortlisting to run this process, resulting in duplicated effort, missed deadlines
and inconsistent communication.
Students often have no single readiness profile, skill-gap analysis or personalised guidance
on which recruiters or roles they best fit, while recruiters struggle to receive well-matched,
pre-screened candidate pools.
Placement drives frequently clash in scheduling, offer letters and joining statuses are tracked
manually, and placement cells have little to no analytics on which branches, skills or
preparation efforts actually convert into offers.
There is therefore a need for an AI-powered, end-to-end Campus-to-Corporate Placement
Management System that can digitise, automate and intelligently optimise the entire
placement lifecycle.
Challenge
Design and develop an AI-powered Campus-to-Corporate Placement Management
System capable of managing student readiness, matching students to recruiter
requirements, scheduling placement drives and generating actionable placement
analytics in near real time.
The proposed system should use technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine
Learning, Natural Language Processing, recommendation systems or Generative AI to
analyse student profiles, recruiter requirements and historical placement data to streamline
the placement process.
The system should not merely digitise records. It should also provide:
● Student readiness and employability scoring
● Skill-gap analysis against target job roles
● Recruiter-student matching and ranking
● Conflict-free drive scheduling
● Offer, documentation and joining-status tracking
● Predictive analytics on placement outcomes
Key Placement Management Areas
Teams may address a combination of the following:
1. Student Readiness Profiling
Build a comprehensive, continuously updated profile for every student covering:
● Resume and academic records
● Certifications and projects
● Aptitude and mock-interview scores
● Communication and soft-skill assessments
● Branch, CGPA and backlog history
The system should identify indicators such as skill gaps, low readiness scores and areas
needing improvement before a student is put forward to a recruiter.
2. Recruiter Requirement Matching
Parse recruiter job descriptions and eligibility criteria, then identify or assess the best-fit
candidates by:
● Skill and technology-stack matching
● Branch and CGPA eligibility filtering
● Certification and project relevance
● Past interview performance
The system may generate a fit/match score and highlight the reasons a student was or was
not shortlisted.
3. Drive Scheduling & Conflict Management
Detect and resolve scheduling issues such as:
● Overlapping company drives on the same date/slot
● Venue and resource double-booking
● Students shortlisted for multiple simultaneous drives
● Interview-panel and infrastructure availability
4. Offer & Documentation Tracking
Track the complete post-selection lifecycle, including:
● Offer letters and CTC details
● Joining bonds, documentation and verification status
● PPOs (pre-placement offers) and internship conversions
● Offer acceptance, deferral or withdrawal
5. Analytics & Predictive Insights
Generate insights such as:
● Branch-wise and skill-wise placement conversion rates
● Salary and package trends across recruiters
● Students at risk of remaining unplaced
● Recruiter engagement and repeat-hiring patterns
6. Communication & Notification Automation
Replace manual email/WhatsApp coordination with automated, targeted notifications for:
● Shortlisting and interview schedules
● Document submission deadlines
● Offer status updates
● Drive announcements and eligibility criteria
Expected Solution
The proposed prototype should preferably contain the following modules:
A. Multi-Source Data Integration Engine
The system may integrate and analyse:
● Student academic and resume data
● Recruiter job descriptions and eligibility criteria
● Placement drive calendars
● Historical placement records
● Assessment and mock-interview results
B. AI-Based Matching & Scoring Engine
Participants may use:
● Machine Learning
● Natural Language Processing
● Recommendation Systems
● Large Language Models
● Predictive Analytics
● Rule + AI Hybrid Models
C. Readiness & Fit Scoring
Every student-recruiter pairing should be assigned an appropriate readiness/fit level, for
example:
Not Ready → Developing → Ready → Highly Employable
The system should explain the major factors behind the score.
D. Explainable Recommendations
Instead of providing only:
“Not Shortlisted”
the system should provide an explanation such as:
“Below Threshold: The student's CGPA meets the eligibility criteria, but the required
skill set shows a gap in cloud technologies and the mock-interview score is below
the recruiter's expected benchmark.”
For recruiter-side recommendations, the system may similarly explain why a candidate pool
was ranked in a particular order.
E. Intelligent Workflow Automation
Depending on the placement stage, the system may recommend or automate:
● Auto-scheduling of drives around detected conflicts
● Auto-shortlisting based on eligibility and fit score
● Personalised preparation recommendations for students
● Automated reminders for pending documentation
● Escalation of at-risk/unplaced students to mentors
● Recruiter follow-up prompts for pending offers
F. Placement Command Dashboard
Develop an interactive dashboard displaying:
● Total students registered and placement-ready
● Active and upcoming drives
● Offers made, accepted and pending
● Branch-wise and skill-wise conversion rates
● Average and highest package trends
● Students at risk of remaining unplaced
● Recruiter pipeline and engagement status
● Documentation and joining-status summary
Innovation Opportunities
Participants are encouraged to explore advanced capabilities such as:
● Generative AI-assisted resume and profile building
● AI-powered mock interview and communication-skill analysis
● Chatbot-based FAQ and eligibility assistant for students
● Predictive at-risk-student identification
● Recruiter-student matching using embeddings/vector search
● Explainable AI for shortlisting decisions
● Real-time drive-conflict detection
● Blockchain-based offer-letter verification
● Integration with LinkedIn/job-portal APIs
● Gamified skill-building and preparation recommendations
● Mobile app for real-time alerts and updates
● Multi-campus/multi-cell analytics aggregation
Minimum Expected Prototype
Each team should demonstrate at least three core placement-management capabilities, of
which preferably:
1. One should involve student readiness profiling and skill-gap analysis.
2. One should involve recruiter-student matching and drive scheduling.
3. One should involve analytics/predictive insights and offer tracking.
The prototype should demonstrate:
Profiling → Matching → Scheduling → Notification → Offer Tracking → Analytics
Minimum Deliverables
Teams should submit/demonstrate:
1. Working prototype.
2. Student readiness/employability scoring mechanism.
3. Recruiter-student matching for at least three simulated drives.
4. Conflict-aware drive scheduling mechanism.
5. Explainable shortlisting/matching output.
6. Placement monitoring dashboard.
7. Offer and documentation tracking mechanism.
8. System architecture.
9. Details of models/algorithms used.
10. Demonstration using simulated or publicly available placement/recruitment
datasets.
11. Accuracy/performance evaluation of matching and scoring.
12. Scalability and deployment approach across multiple campuses.
Desired Impact
The proposed solution should have the potential to evolve into an intelligent Campus-toCorporate Placement Ecosystem that can be deployed across university and college
placement cells. The objective is to create a system that improves placement outcomes for
students, simplifies recruiter engagement, and gives placement cells data-driven visibility
and control over the entire hiring pipeline.

What is Campus Link? It is an "intelligent bridge" designed to connect students, colleges, and recruiters to organize the entire placement journey intelligently and efficiently [04:12].

Evaluating Beyond Academic Scores: When asked if AI simply judges students by their marks, Sandeep clarifies that it builds a comprehensive "readiness profile." This includes technical skills, internships, projects, and aptitude, offering a holistic view of a student's true potential rather than just an academic snapshot [05:07].

Constructive Rejection and Skill Gaps: If a student lacks the skills for a specific job, the platform doesn't just reject them. It identifies the exact gap (like a missing technical skill or communication competency), allowing the student to understand what they must improve upon for future opportunities [06:10].

Matching Candidates to Jobs: The platform leverages Natural Language Processing (NLP) to read and interpret recruiter job descriptions. It then matches those required criteria against student profiles to recommend the most aligned candidates [07:46].

Explainable AI Scores: If a student gets an 80% suitability score for a role, the AI doesn't leave them guessing. It provides an explanation showing exactly which requirements they met and highlights where the 20% gap lies [08:52].

Opportunities for Non-Toppers: The system helps average students by evaluating diverse parameters. Because different companies value different traits (e.g., strong projects or communication over purely academic scores), Campus Link highlights capable candidates who might otherwise be overlooked [09:49].

Fixing Chaotic Schedules and Missed Messages: The platform acts as a centralized communication and scheduling hub. It automates schedules to avoid conflicts between exams and interviews [11:28], and prevents students from missing crucial updates that easily get lost in emails or WhatsApp groups [12:20].

Post-Placement Support: Campus Link doesn't stop working once a student is hired. It continues to track them for about 18 months. If a student faces layoffs or issues early in their career, the system helps find them alternative opportunities [13:27].

Identifying "At-Risk" Students: The AI identifies students who consistently show gaps in aptitude or technical skills. This is not meant to label them as failures, but to flag them so colleges can provide extra mentoring and training before the placement season begins [15:25].

Data-Driven Curriculum Changes: If platform analytics reveal that a large number of students lack a specific skill that recruiters frequently demand, colleges can use this data to introduce new training programs or courses [16:29].

Data Privacy: Handling sensitive student and recruiter data requires strict access controls. Sandeep emphasizes that the platform is built with responsible privacy policies and security as a core foundation [17:41].

The Biggest Development Challenge: Building the AI model was only part of the challenge. Ensuring the platform fit naturally into the real-world workflow of college placement officers was equally critical to its success [18:45].

The 3-to-5 Year Vision: Sandeep envisions Campus Link evolving from a simple placement management tool into a massive digital ecosystem that actively guides students as they transition from campus to the corporate world [19:40].

Conclusion: The host concludes that AI is not here to replace human placement teams, but to empower students, colleges, and recruiters with better data. The episode ends with the regional sign-off, "Jay Jagannath" [21:18].
